# Claim register for outside tools and companies (deliverable 8)

Date: 2026-10-28. Source: the Gemini review (`workflow discussion and review - gemini.md`, 549 lines, five separate responses). A model extracted every factual assertion with a verbatim quote (3 calls, 178 anchored claims, 99% of quotes found at the cited line); duplicates were merged to 174 claims. 67 are about outside companies, tools, model capabilities or prices; the rest are opinions or claims about the Librarian project's own files and measurements.

**Tags.** *verified*: confirmed by a primary source fetched in this sprint (URL and retrieval time in `basis`) or by direct observation on this machine (stated in `basis`). *plausible*: consistent secondary reports or local data, no primary source retrieved. *unverified*: no source. Result for the 58 genuinely outside claims: **3 verified (1 on a fetched source, 2 on direct observation), 22 plausible, 33 unverified**; a further 9 were filed as outside claims by the extractor but concern the project's own data and were checked against it (tag `local`). Unverified is the default for claims I did not individually check, so the large unverified count measures effort, not falsity. 3 of the unverified claims conflict with the sources retrieved (CL-010, CL-022, CL-023); the conflict is stated in `basis` and the tag stays unverified. The rules that assigned tags are in `scripts/build_claim_register.py`; they were written by hand. Spot-check of 2026-10-28: every verified row and every row matched by the broad patterns (Codex, groups and swarms, Lean, the cap and provisioning figures) was re-read against its basis. That changed one tag (CL-025, verified to plausible, because the primary README gives 721 and no initial count) and two basis lines (provisioning minutes, validation cost). The default-unverified rows were not re-read one by one.

**Rule applied.** No design in `06-design-proposals.md` depends on a plausible or unverified claim. The outside facts the designs rest on are: DeepInfra's live model and price listing (verified), and fields in Claude Code and Codex logs (measured locally). The OpenAI figures are used only to evaluate Gemini's review.
