Score = 2 x best category + sum of categories + domain bonus (yes +2, partial +0.5, no -3) - 2 for a keyword trap + 1 if the evidence quote was found at its cited page, -1 if not. Ties favour papers with a method or experiment. The one-line reasons are model-written; for the top 18 they were corroborated by anchor-verified method cards from two readers (`04-extraction-table.md`). A paper that scores zero everywhere is a keyword match on something else (for example "drift" in class-incremental learning, "cooperation" among UAVs, "agent" in scheduling), not a candidate.
