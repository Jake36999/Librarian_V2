## Found in addition to the review
1. The Claude per-call delegation records the first pass called missing are in the session scratchpad: 25 `delegate.py` ledger rows ($0.5457, the same figure as the aggregated import) and 20 `second_reader.py` logs (about $0.45, in no ledger). Agents' recorded DeepInfra spend over the five days is about $1.15, not $0.70.
2. The repository runner refuses `--max-output-tokens` above 16,384; this bounds how much one call can return, which matters for any extraction design.
3. A sprint-session filter was missing from the compaction count (it briefly doubled the rate); fixed.
4. The support audit found that the page cited for a method anchors its mechanism quote, not its result text: all 596 numbers in 166 result texts appear in the paper, but for 81% of the primary reader's items at least one is on another page. The tables now say so. Two of the five items I spot-checked as supported describe other methods in related work, which the extraction schema did not distinguish from the paper's own.
5. The sprint's numbers moved when other sessions about this sprint (the review itself, this revision) added to the live Codex and Claude logs; the baseline is now cut at the moment the first pass was frozen (`DATA_CUTOFF` in `analyze_baseline.py`), and the determinism check compares two consecutive runs on the same inputs.
