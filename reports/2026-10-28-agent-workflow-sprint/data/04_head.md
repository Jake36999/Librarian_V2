# Methods extracted from the literature (deliverable 3, part 2, revision 2)

Date: 2026-10-28 (revised the same evening after a second-pass review; the first-pass table is in `revision-1/`). Papers are cited by their first-pass rank (#n, see `03-reading-list.md`), section heading and PDF page.

**What changed.** The first pass triaged papers on their first and last pages, read 18 of them with a cap of six method cards each, and its table drew on 13 papers: it left even the highly ranked Embodied Tree of Thoughts without a row and omitted reported costs, negative results and limitations. Revision 2 reads **all @@N_PAPERS@@ distinct papers in full**, one paper per call, with a fixed checklist: problem, evidence type, sample and unit, comparators, methods (categories A to E), costs the paper reports, negative results, limitations and reproducibility notes, each with a page and a verbatim quote. Category E, verification, grounding and deterministic checking, is new: the first pass's A to D had nowhere to put work whose subject is checking an agent's output. The reading produced @@ITEMS_VERIFIED@@ anchor-verified items (@@ITEMS_BY_FIELD@@).

**How to read the tables.** Each row is an item the primary reader extracted whose quote a script found on the cited PDF page; the item, its section and page are copied from the data, so the citation is exact. The page cited is the page of the mechanism quote; the **Result** text is the reader's summary from anywhere in the paper (see the reader audit), so a result may sit on another page. The last column, **Applies to Librarian (Hypothesis)**, is mine and is not something the paper claims. The tables are a curated selection of @@N_CURATED@@ rows (`data/lit2_curation.csv`); every extracted item, with its quote, is in `data/lit2_items.csv`, and the per-paper checklist is in `data/lit2_papers.csv`.

**What the checks show and do not show** (details in the reader audit at the end). A script confirms each quote sits on its cited page. That proves the evidence exists, not that the item supports the label, and it says nothing about methods the reader did not select. A support audit of @@AUDIT_N@@ sampled items and a coverage check against an independent checklist address those two gaps, with limits stated there.

**Evidence limits.** Results are the authors' own, from simulations, benchmarks, case studies or surveys. None evaluates delegation by a closed-weight lead to cheaper models on software work, so the literature supplies metrics, schemas and cautions but no validated cost model for our setting. Surveys (#04, #10, #13, #21) report other people's results; their headline savings (for example #04's "up to 89%") are not experiments. Papers #01 and #12 share authors, method and benchmark and are not independent replications. The vault notes on ReAct, Reflexion, Voyager and similar papers were written from memory and are secondary sources; the primary Reflexion and Tree of Thoughts papers were not retrieved in this sprint (a second-pass review cites them, and I could not check those anchors). Applicability levels are the reader's judgement from the paper's content.
