# Open questions and the experiments that would answer them (deliverable 6, revision 2)

Date: 2026-10-28 (revised the same evening after a second-pass review; the first-pass file is in `revision-1/`). Each question says what we do not know, why the existing evidence cannot settle it, and the cheapest experiment that would. Costs are DeepInfra dollars at live prices plus rough orchestrator effort. The second-pass review asked that experiments be designed around the quantity they claim to measure; E1 to E4 were rewritten for that, and E11 to E13 are new.

**Design rules for every experiment.** A pair means both treatments on the same baseline task, with order randomised and earlier outputs hidden. Count packet preparation, delegate work, checking, repair and failed outcomes, not only the delegate's call. Report paired differences with uncertainty clustered by task. Precision alone rewards a reviewer that reports almost nothing, so reviewers are scored on known defects (recall and severity), verified-clean changes (false-positive burden) and precision together.
