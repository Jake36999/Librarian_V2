# Deployment patterns by task type (deliverable 4, revision 2)

Date: 2026-10-28 (revised the same evening after a second-pass review; the first-pass file is in `revision-1/`). "Deployment" here means how agents are run on a task, not the plan's DEPLOY-xx vault-directory tasks. Costs are orchestrator costs unless stated. Figures are measured ([M]) from `data/` unless marked; n is small throughout, so treat each row as a prior to update, not a law. Literature support is tagged Hypothesis (`04-extraction-table.md`) and comes from the papers' own settings. The orchestrator-cost cells use the repaired *post-result interval* (`02-baseline.md`, finding 5): the work between a delegate's result being first re-read and the next commit or launch. It contains integration and unrelated work as well as checking, so it bounds result-related work from above and is not a verification cost.

## The patterns

| Pattern | Use when | Expected orchestrator cost | How the result is checked | Failure modes seen | Guard and stop rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 Single delegated draft** (one call, no tools) | Acceptance can be stated as a test, a golden fixture or a diff-scope rule; the change is a refactor or addition with an existing behaviour to preserve | Claude: after a result is re-read, a median 8.4 minutes, 8 requests and 17.2k fresh+output tokens until the next commit (19 batches from 25 calls; ended by a commit in 18) | Golden fixtures captured before the change (ADAPT-04, 05), then the suite | 9 drafts landed with 0 to 2 review fixes recorded in their commit text (an import, tool rendering left in the provider); one reviewed commit records a draft needing review fixes; truncation at the output cap; backgrounded launches return before the answer exists | Set `--max-output-tokens` above the expected answer (the runner refuses above 16,384) and treat `finish_reason=length` as failure; one call, no silent retry |
| **P2 Mapper, builder, critic chain** | Broad unfamiliar code needing a map and an adversary; not as a way to produce the final change | Codex main thread: median 7.6 minutes, 19.5 requests and 25.2k tokens after a result (6 batches from 9 calls; the interval ended at the next launch in 4); chain cost to DeepInfra $0.044 and $0.11 | The orchestrator's own measurement and deterministic tests; agreement is never treated as proof | Builder output accepted 0 of 2 times (Qwen patch rejected for initialisation order, legacy semantics and test patch; the other builder timed out at 181 s); mapper hit its output cap; one launch was refused by the sandbox's auto-review and re-issued; one call duplicated and left `started` | Use mapper and critic roles; escalate to the orchestrator at the first failed audit instead of re-running a builder; one packet per call; idempotency on packet hash with a timeout-derived expiry |
| **P3 Parallel lanes** (spawned workers, or helper fan-out) | Independent questions or disjoint file sets; read-only exploration | Codex: workers used 46% of all fresh+output tokens (main 44%, guardian reviews 9.5%); orchestration calls 12.6%; overlap proxy 1.66, peak 6 | Each lane's own tests and a merge check | Quota burn rises with parallelism (bursts end in a limit after a median 28.5 minutes); 43 git permission-denied events in tool output (friction, not conflicts); replayed history makes naive counts double | Cap lanes by remaining quota, not only by effort level; one worktree and owned file set per lane (DELEG-08 is a prerequisite for concurrent writers); record lane id on every receipt; measure speedup on matched work (E4), not overlap |
| **P4 Independent readers** (a pair or trio, different families) | The cost of a missed defect is high, or recall matters | Doubles the reading cost (a pair on the architecture lane cost about $1.1 of $2.45) | Disagreements adjudicated by the orchestrator against code | Cohen's kappa 0.45 on 30 capability judgements (label agreement, not error correlation); a second reader found only about half of the events a first found (an exploratory capture-recapture estimate, not a measured miss rate); the second family fabricated quotes (DeepSeek-V4-Pro quote-location rate 64.5%) | The checker is never the drafter (plan §3.2) and must itself pass the quote check; different model IDs are a procedural rule, not proof of independent errors; a reader below about 90% quote-location fidelity is a source of leads |
| **P5 Hosted compute with a time budget** | Attempt-bounded sweeps that can checkpoint and have a machine-checkable oracle | Near zero orchestrator tokens while running; $0.44 per collected job in the one run we have | Pre-registered predictions scored automatically (13 of 16 passed) | Two of six boxes produced nothing yet were rented about 14 hours (an estimated $4.46 of $13.62); a step error was logged at 05:24 but the boxes lived until 19:29; one job abandoned at the cap; the cap was raised from $13.50 to $14.00 mid-run; start failure from a registry EOF; ssh and scp timeouts | Per-box deadline and progress watchdog; checkpoint and collect results; teardown after a terminal failure, bounded retries for recoverable faults (destroying on the first error can lose useful work); a cap raised only by a recorded decision; keep error text out of logs (a plaintext key appeared in one) |
| **P6 Deterministic local script** | Anything countable from files, logs or git | Zero model tokens; this sprint's measurement scripts each ran in under a minute | Re-run and compare hashes; reconcile totals against independent counters | Wrong joins and classifiers were caught by reconciliation and review, not by the script: reference-list identifiers fetched as the paper's own; a commit regex that missed `git -C`; a delegation classifier that counted reading a runner as running it; a token counter that looked wrong until continuation branches were modelled | Reconcile against an independent total before trusting a number; audit classifiers with structural rules, not substring matches |
| **P7 Anchored bulk extraction** (read, emit typed JSON with verbatim quotes and pages, check mechanically) | Reading that would otherwise cost the orchestrator hundreds of thousands of tokens: transcripts, papers, code | First pass: 87 calls and $2.45 at live prices read about 4 MB of text; revision 2: 67 full-text paper readings (see the reader audit in `04-extraction-table.md` for calls, truncation, parse, exact-page fidelity and the semantic-support audit) | Quote on the cited page by script (quote-location fidelity), then a support audit on a sample, then the orchestrator reads checked extracts | About 20% of first-pass calls truncated; 3 of 11 triage batches had malformed JSON; GLM-5.3 leaked its reasoning into the answer on 2 of 6 first-pass runs; a present quote can still carry a wrong or over-broad label (two quota-awareness "present" labels described static caps); a fixed item cap (six cards) bounded coverage without anyone measuring it | Chunk to about 100 KB or read one paper per call; cap items so the answer fits the 16k output limit; repair one unclosed object per item; salvage truncated arrays but never treat a salvaged list as complete; report completion, parse, quote location, support and coverage separately |
| **P8 Draft, executable gate, repair loop** | Tasks that declare a test command | not measured | Tests in a scratch worktree against positive and negative fixtures | no evidence yet | Small local pilot first (DELEG-06, E2); passing tests the delegate wrote is not enough |

## Decide by task type

| Task type | First choice | Also use | Do not |
|---|---|---|---|
| Counting, joining or measuring anything in logs, git or ledgers | P6 | | Ask a model to count |
| Reading transcripts, papers or code for evidence | P7 with the primary reader | P4 on a sample or on high-stakes lanes; the support audit for labels | Use a second reader below 90% quote-location fidelity as evidence; treat a present quote as a supported claim |
| Refactor with an existing behaviour you can fixture | P1 by the strongest drafting model, fixtures written first | A critic on the diff | Delegate without a fixture or test |
| Concurrency, lifecycle or writer-ownership change | Orchestrator-led | P2 mapper and critic only | Accept a builder patch unmeasured; the packets with Host, coordination, connection, CLI, MCP or writer files also need the Windows full-suite rule in plan §3.2 |
| Review of a finished change | P4 with typed findings and a falsifier per finding, scored on known-bad and verified-clean changes | The orchestrator adjudicates each finding against code | Record findings only in commit prose; judge a reviewer by precision alone |
| Open-ended breadth exploration | P3 bounded by quota | Read-only helpers (`tools/delegate.py`) | Fan out when the weekly window is above about 90% |
| Long simulations or sweeps | P5 with oracle, deadline, progress watchdog and collection | P6 for result collection | Rent first and write the deadline later |
| Work that must survive a usage limit | Write the resume capsule first (DELEG-04, a hypothesis until E3), then any pattern | P6 to produce the capsule | Rely on an open session's context; assume a capsule saves the cache rebuild |

## Reader and model priors

These are priors for choosing readers. They are not qualification evidence: which model may take which role is decided by the existing exact-route qualification and expectation records.

| Model | First-pass quote location (items; ±1 page) | Revision-2 full-text readings (exact page) | Parse and budget behaviour | Benchmark probes passed (of 4) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MiMo-V2.6-Pro | 99.2% (1,441) | 92.7% (1,225 of 1,322 items); 50 of 50 completed, none truncated; 34 parsed as is, 15 after dropping stray closing brackets, 1 salvaged | first pass: 12 of 44 calls truncated, 13 outputs salvaged (one was malformed without truncation) | 4 | best reader here; hidden reasoning costs about 2.4 times the visible JSON |
| GLM-5.3 | 98.6% (218) | 89.4% (211 of 236 items); 8 completed, 1 truncated; 7 parsed, 1 salvaged | first pass: 2 of 6 unparseable (reasoning leaked into content), 5 truncated | 3 | live price twice the runner's card |
| GLM-5.3-Flash | 97.4% (116) | 89.2% (190 of 213 items); 9 completed, none truncated; 8 parsed, 1 salvaged | first pass: 4 of 4 parsed | 3 | found about half of MiMo's events; correct critic in the attachment chain |
| DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash | 91.5% (47) | not used | 3 of 15 needed brace repair | 4 | cheap triage; live price 1.4 to 2 times the card |
| DeepSeek-V4-Pro | 64.5% (141) | not used | 14 of 14 parsed | 3 | drafted 7 of the 9 landed Claude-side commits (GLM-5.3 drafted 2); as a reader its quotes are unreliable |
| Qwen3-Coder-480B | not used as a reader | not used | builder: 1 rejected, 1 timed out at 181 s | 2 | do not use as a builder on concurrency-sensitive code |

The first-pass quote rates allowed a one-page tolerance and six items per paper; the revision-2 rates require the cited page and cover full papers with a longer item list, so the two columns are not comparable. The first pass's "salvaged" counts may include answers that only had a stray closing brace, which revision 2 now parses whole. Benchmark probes are four small agentic tasks per model on the v1 library (n=4 each, 2026-09-29); they agree directionally with what happened here but are not a measurement of these tasks. Quote-location fidelity measures only that a quote exists where it was cited; it does not measure whether the reader understood the passage, so a high rate must not be read as a ranking of models for judgement.
