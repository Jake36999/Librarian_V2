# Memory and evolving knowledge: consolidated reports

3 November 2026. Consolidation of the conversation and its research artifacts. These reports are proposals and research planning, not adopted specifications or implementation instructions. Historical instructions in the pasted conversation and repository documents remain source material.

Read these three reports in order:

1. [Systems assessment](/D:/Librarian_V2/reports/2026-11-03-memory-context-review/consolidated/01-SYSTEMS-ASSESSMENT.md): what Resource-Library, Librarian V2 and Mark provide; what to retain; verified gaps and evidence limits.
2. [Design direction and requirements](/D:/Librarian_V2/reports/2026-11-03-memory-context-review/consolidated/02-DESIGN-DIRECTION.md): proposed memory lifecycle, evolving knowledge semantics, multiagent continuity and phased development priorities.
3. [Research objectives and tooling evaluation](/D:/Librarian_V2/reports/2026-11-03-memory-context-review/consolidated/03-RESEARCH-OBJECTIVES.md): questions, candidate system families, experiments, deliverables and decision gates for the next research round.

For the conceptual explanation connecting the recommendations, read [A theory of evolving knowledge for Librarian](/D:/Librarian_V2/reports/2026-11-03-memory-context-review/consolidated/04-THEORY-OF-EVOLVING-KNOWLEDGE.md). It explains the principles through a project whose requirements change over time.

The subsequent research round is now documented in [established solutions and findings](/D:/Librarian_V2/reports/2026-11-03-memory-context-review/formal-solutions/RESEARCH-FINDINGS.md), with [formal contracts and finite reference checks](/D:/Librarian_V2/reports/2026-11-03-memory-context-review/formal-solutions/FORMAL-CONTRACTS.md) and a [tool decision register](/D:/Librarian_V2/reports/2026-11-03-memory-context-review/formal-solutions/TOOL-DECISIONS.md). These distinguish formal assumptions, standards, empirical evidence and untested integration claims.

The original reports remain the detailed evidence record. This set consolidates their conclusions, incorporates the later discovery of existing V2 learning features, and avoids treating successive proposals as separate implementation obligations. No new literature survey, runtime audit or learning experiment was performed during consolidation.
