## Existing foundations and remaining formalism gaps

The [implementation plan's work-packet section](D:/Librarian_V2/docs/unified-implementation-plan.md:169) already asks for task IDs, objectives, scope, preserved behavior, exact commands, acceptance tests, owners and budgets. Its [development lane section](D:/Librarian_V2/docs/unified-implementation-plan.md:145) also requires relevant Windows behavior to be checked on the owner's host. Those are current local requirements, not an academic discovery.

The current [development runner](D:/Librarian_V2/scripts/delegate_deepinfra.py:1) provides constrained export, file/packet hashes, reservations, atomic receipts and one explicit request per invocation. It warns that output is untrusted and must be applied and validated locally. Its `completed` status denotes completion of the model call; a length stop has a warning, not semantic task failure. A future job controller should preserve that distinction rather than changing the meaning of past receipts.

| Gap | Operational meaning | Appropriate evidence |
|---|---|---|
| “A task” lacks a consistent unit | One draft, a tested patch and a reviewed integrated change currently appear in similar prose. | Revisioned objective, artifact boundary and acceptance method. |
| “Done” conflates different states | Provider response, usable artifact, verified behavior and integration differ. | Recorded transitions with command results and artifact identities. |
| “Verified” conflates different claims | Exact quote, supported interpretation, adequate coverage and actual executable behavior differ. | Distinct checks, uncertainty and ownership of each verdict. |
| “Independent review” is underspecified | Different model names can share context, assumptions and failure modes. | Hold back acceptance fixtures; challenge concrete claims; adjudicate against source or execution. |
| “Saved work” has no comparable denominator | Tokens returned or files changed do not establish lead effort avoided. | Comparable direct-work arm and full rescue/verification accounting. |
| “Parallel” has no dependency model | Overlapping requests can contend, duplicate work or edit stale state. | Artifact ownership, dependency-aware admission and measured fixed-work completion. |

For a first deployment, none of this requires a general theorem prover, a new knowledge graph, a full event bus or an app-wide service redesign. Small executable contracts and transparent lifecycle records can make the assumptions testable.

## DeepInfra participation and limits

The five transcripts were partitioned into 13 bounded reading packets. MiMo-V2.6-Pro extracted episodes with short quotes and source lines. Local normalized quote checks rejected four of 104 proposed episode anchors; 100 passed. Passing anchors retain the label **UNADJUDICATED** in the raw worker dataset. They establish text presence, not correct role attribution, truth or completeness. This report uses the selected, locally examined cases above, not a quantitative aggregation of those model labels.

Notable limits of the automated reader were visible without hidden ground truth: it sometimes classified Gemini's retrospective assertions as observed workflows, attributed work inside quoted conversations according to the outer filename, or described direct lead work as a worker contribution. Rejected anchors are preserved, not silently repaired into machine-verified evidence. These limitations support the report's separation of mechanical anchoring from semantic adjudication.

Automatic approval review blocked a proposed export of the full private draft and selected transcript excerpts, stating that the specific sensitive export lacked authorization. That export did not execute and was not retried. The safer remote critique used only [a newly authored generic contract](D:/Librarian_V2/reports/2026-10-28-deepinfra-overseer-workflow/data/critic_public_contract.txt), containing no transcripts, source code or local findings. Consequently it can challenge the contract's logic; it cannot independently fact-check this report. The [approval record](D:/Librarian_V2/reports/2026-10-28-deepinfra-overseer-workflow/data/approval_review.json) preserves the boundary.

The abstract critic returned seven findings. Their locally adjudicated [dispositions](D:/Librarian_V2/reports/2026-10-28-deepinfra-overseer-workflow/data/critic_dispositions.json) strengthened acceptance protection, environment identity, uncertain-spend handling, initial audit effort and cross-lead measurement. The claim that the accounting identity assumes independent or constant overhead was rejected; sensitivity was nevertheless made clearer. A suggested provider-billing lookup was not assumed to exist. This is another example of useful criticism requiring adjudication.

All **14 calls completed for an estimated $0.35728741**, exactly reconciled to the local charged ledger at eight decimal places. There were 13 reading calls and one abstract critique; none remains pending or unknown. Receipts and raw outputs are preserved in the [archive index](D:/Librarian_V2/reports/2026-10-28-deepinfra-overseer-workflow/data/call_archive.json). Its low worker API bill does not demonstrate an achieved 20/80 split or the proposed controller's reliability. No lead-effort saving was measured.
