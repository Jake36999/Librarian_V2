## 1. Design Principles & Non-Regression Mandate

Reconstructing the project from V1 (`DOC-V1D-*`), V2 policies (`DOC-POL-01..18`), finalization plans (`DOC-PLN-01..10`, `DOC-RPT-04*`), live `d9435e1` runtime slices, and `G:\Libraries\Resource-Library` observations yields **five non-negotiable invariants** that any refined "to-be" pipeline must preserve:

1. **Maintain the Single Consolidated Airlock (`ingest` + `StagingStore`) Without Collapsing Grades (`DOC-V1D-12`, `DOC-V1D-20`, `DOC-V1D-30`, `DOC-POL-07`):** V2 consolidated V1's fragmented frontdoors (`build_solutions_library.py`, `scout`, `papers`, `enrich`) into `intake.ingest`, `.librarian/staging/`, `batch.start`, and `promote`. Consolidation governs the *state pipeline*; it must **never** collapse Bronze capture, Silver preview (`describe`), Silver full enrichment (`STAGES`/`FOLDER_STAGES`), and Silver catalog acceptance (`promote`) into one unreviewed hop.
2. **Enforce Evidence-Derived Medallion Replay & DIK Isolation (`DOC-V1D-05`, `DOC-V1D-08`, `DOC-V1D-44`, `DOC-POL-06`):** Every **Silver** store (`.librarian/conversions/`, `.librarian/staging/`, promoted `Sources/`, `index.sqlite`, `Indexes/`, `.nodes.json`, Geo DBs) must remain reproducible from immutable **Bronze** inputs (`.evidence/`, `quarantine/`, `queued` inputs, `folder-surveys.json`) and strictly **unframed** (`DATA_IS_UNFRAMED`), while keeping project need/pragmatics inside **Gold** (`Session`, `Brief`, `Offerings/`, `Applications/`). Storage Medallion grades remain orthogonal to Trust Tiers (`gold`/`silver`/`bronze` in `DOC-POL-02`, `DOC-POL-09`) and permission tiers (`consult`/`contribute`/`curate`).
3. **Preserve Strict Actor & Decision Separation (`DOC-POL-02`, `DOC-POL-03`):** Deterministic **Core** owns hashing, storage, locking, queue bookkeeping, structural validation, and script execution. **Clerk** (`tier 2`) and **Scribe** (`tier 3`) own unframed semantic interpretation, neutral drafting, and grounded synthesis. **Lead** (`tier 1`) owns orchestration within explicit grant; **Tier 0** represents an **external, non-Librarian harness/agent using the Librarian to support another task** (at `contribute` / explicit accepted `F-03` grant — never forced into internal Librarian babysitting or blocked on `curate`-only Owner prompts); **Owner** (`curate` / person) retains promotion, sensitivity, taxonomy, budget-extension, folder acceptance, and reserved decisions, shielded from routine work by **Lead**, **Oracle**, and **Doctor**; **Oracle** provides two-stage independent screening & verification; **Doctor** executes only *accepted* YAML remedies inside bounded blast radius without ever auto-rolling back or self-closing tickets.
4. **Preserve Already-Landed `d9435e1` Fixes (`DOC-PLN-04`, `DOC-RPT-04`):** Any to-be design must retain chunk-digest `MF-013` reuse (`deep_read.py:256-368`, `RECORD_VERSION = 2`), `MF-014` attachment SHA-256 tracking (`index.py:452-499`), data-driven `phases.yaml` (`W1a`, `F-06`/`F-12`, `@graph_*` research injection), balanced `spread` sampling (`deep_read.py:939-952`), blind E4/E5 lens verification, `PromotionGuard` 200-file CAS, and `tools/intake.py:103-147` `_framing` restoration for brief-scoped queues.
5. **No Clean Regression via Aesthetic Oversimplification:** Removing complexity on paper while hiding real failure branches, budget races (`MC-10`), Geo-Grapher boundary failures (`OP2`, `0`-claim graphs, `0` bridges), `Unfiled` taxonomy stagnation (`W8`), or Staging pagination limits (`F-01`) would reproduce the exact blind spots documented in `DOC-MAP-13` and `DOC-RPT-10`.
