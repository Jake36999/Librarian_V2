Classifications used:
- **`match`**: Pack aligns with original specs, `docs/policy/`, and current implementation.

- **`unimplemented-in-code`**: Pack design (to-be runtime/UI/Oracle/Doctor/Ticket behavior) not yet built in `383a3d1`.
