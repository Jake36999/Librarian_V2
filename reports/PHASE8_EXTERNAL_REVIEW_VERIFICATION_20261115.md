1. Inventory already has folder_intake.inventory_record, EvidenceStore and DIK integration. Old conformance tables cannot establish current absence. Validate actual emitted inventory payload and revision binding.
2. Code AST extraction can populate structural graphs independently of paid semantic inference; papers need persistent page-mapped source representation for the paper surface. These have different prerequisites.
3. Claims/concepts projection is a genuine gap: current exported section arrays are empty. Connect verified locators and structured read data, not a second speculative extraction.
4. Use rollups already enter paper export; community-to-Geo synchronization and automatic contextual bridges are not established implementations. Define their ownership, refresh and review semantics first.
5. Publication currently happens in parse when full_conversion exists, before later read/review stages. This is the sequencing issue: distinguish structural/unverified availability from verified semantic publication, or defer the verified binding. A graph may exist without proving a source claim.

## Full-page optical ingestion refinement

Owner direction on 2026-11-15 identifies missing visual and mathematical content when text-bearing pages bypass optical processing. Full PDF ingestion must inspect every page visually, including pages with a text layer. Persist page images or reproducible original-page locators, faithful prose and table transcription, LaTeX for equations, and separate labelled descriptions of figures, diagrams, axes, legends and visible relationships. A description is generated interpretation and carries uncertainty and page/region provenance. Keep visual descriptions distinct from verbatim source transcription. Retain the PDF as the authoritative original for figures and source comparison.

The configured OCR prompt currently asks only for text transcription and cannot establish visual-content coverage. Extend the optical service contract and response validation to cover visual inventory and uncertain symbols explicitly. Preserve existing reading and rendering entry points. Cache hidden per-page results by original digest, page, rendering settings, model/adapter and extraction recipe so retries can reuse completed compatible pages. Record missing or unreadable regions and truncated outputs. Aggregate into hidden Markdown plus structured enrichment records and expose the catalogue summary through the user-facing surface.

Evaluate optical-first processing as the candidate default for full ingestion. A single optical pass may replace mechanical extraction plus text cleanup when it passes coverage and fidelity checks. Existing text extraction can support comparison and search. Cost comparison must include rendered-image input, transcription and visual-description output, reasoning, retries and verification. The built-in one-sentence OCR test establishes basic service operation only. Use representative text, equations, tables and figures in free adapter/response fixtures, then a bounded measured page comparison within the authorized pilot budget before claiming lower cost or scaling to all ten papers. Existing target-state and verification requirements continue to govern completion.
