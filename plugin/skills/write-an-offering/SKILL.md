# Writing an offering

An offering is what a session hands the person: which sources, together, answer the need, and
why. It is built from the candidates you kept in **judge**, and it is checked.

1. `record_synthesis`: the outcome (`offering`, or `nothing` when the kept sources
   do not answer the need), how the
   kept sources fit together, the unknowns, and which to open first.
2. `draft_offering` with a title, a two-line summary, and claims. **Every claim quotes its
   source exactly** (`quote`), from the source's own text; a quote that is not in the text is
   refused (`EVIDENCE_QUOTE_VERIFIED`). Paraphrase in `text`; never in `quote`.
3. `promote_offering`. If the vault's promotion setting needs the person, the result carries a
   question for them; tell the person and wait. Do not answer it yourself.

Write for someone who has never heard of these sources. If two claims could be swapped between
sources without either becoming wrong, both are too generic.
